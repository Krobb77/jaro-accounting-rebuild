Services & Pricing

What services do you offer?

Tax Services — from basic personal income tax to complex corporate structures. Bookkeeping Services — monthly accounting reports, journal and ledger, sales tax reports, and monthly reconciliation. Business Consulting — incorporation, holding companies, and how to pay yourself out of your business. See Our Services for details.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Three monthly packages by transaction volume: the Small Business Package at $149.99/mo (under 20 transactions per month), the Medium Business Package at $249.99/mo (20 to 50 transactions), and the Large Business Package at $299.99/mo (more than 50 transactions). Contact us for a free estimate.

How much does tax filing cost?

Individual tax filing from $99.99; corporate tax filing from $999.99; non-profit organization tax filing from $499.99; nil corporate returns from $349.99.

What is included in the bookkeeping packages?

Monthly accounting reports, journal and ledger, sales tax reports, and monthly reconciliation. Tax filings are priced separately, and one-off projects such as multi-year cleanups are quoted in writing before work starts.

Yes. Contact us now for a free estimate.
