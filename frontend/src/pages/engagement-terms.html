Engagement Terms

When you become a client of Jaro Accounting, we agree on engagement terms in writing before work begins. They’re the plain-language contract for our working relationship: what we’ll do, what you’ll do, and what it costs.

What the terms cover

  • Scope of services — exactly which work is included (bookkeeping, tax preparation, consulting) and what isn’t.
  • Fees and billing — how fees are calculated, when invoices are issued, and payment terms.
  • Your responsibilities — providing complete, timely information and documents so we can do the work properly.
  • Our responsibilities — the standard of care we bring, and how we communicate with you.
  • Confidentiality — your financial information stays yours; we don’t share it without your instruction or a legal obligation.
  • Ending the engagement — how either side can end the working relationship, and what happens to your records.

When you accept them

Clients of the online portal accept the current engagement terms at first sign-in, before accessing any documents or filings. Every acceptance is recorded with a timestamp and the exact version accepted, so there’s never any question about which terms apply.
