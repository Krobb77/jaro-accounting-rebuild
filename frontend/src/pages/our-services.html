Consulting Services

Some of the most valuable conversations we have with business owners start with a question, not a form:

  • Is incorporation right for me? We walk through what incorporation would actually change for your taxes, your liability, and your admin workload — and we tell you honestly when staying unincorporated is the better call.
  • What is a holding company, and do I need one? We explain what holding companies do in plain language, when the structure earns its keep, and when it’s complexity you don’t need yet.
  • What is the best way to pay myself out of my business? Salary, dividends, or a mix — we compare the options against your real numbers so you can choose with the full picture in front of you.

Our consulting work centres on three areas: tax planning, corporate and business structures, and personal tax strategies. Engagements are scoped and quoted individually, because no two businesses need the same advice.
