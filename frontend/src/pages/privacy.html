Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026. Jaro Accounting (1238855 BC LTD), 215 Mowat Street, New Westminster, BC V3M 4B1.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and message — only when you send one.
  • Consultation booking: your name, email address, phone number, and appointment details — only when you book.

This website sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics in its current form. The site is static pages; there are no accounts, and nothing is collected silently in the background.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiries and messages.
  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your consultation booking.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not add you to marketing lists. If we ever want to use your information for anything beyond responding to you, we’ll ask first.

How we protect it

Messages and booking details are handled only by Jaro Accounting staff (currently just Jared). Please don’t include SINs, bank account numbers, or other sensitive identifiers in the contact form — we’ll collect documents securely after your consultation.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and booking records only as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal obligations as an accounting firm, then they’re deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

Under British Columbia’s privacy law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Contact jared@jaroaccounting.com and we’ll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
