About Us

Jaro Accounting exists for one reason: to empower small business owners and driven entrepreneurs across Canada to succeed on their own terms. We build our work on trust and confidence — the kind that comes from straight answers, careful work, and showing up consistently.

We commit to long-lasting relationships with our clients and to a standard of excellence that goes beyond the scope of any single engagement. When your business grows, we grow with it.

Meet the Owner

I’m Jared Robbins — an accountant and tax strategist with nearly a decade of experience helping Canadian businesses keep more of what they earn and plan with confidence.

My practice began as a small bookkeeping business. It has grown into a full-service firm serving more than 300 satisfied clients across industries — from first-year sole proprietors to established incorporated companies.

My specialties include bookkeeping, corporate and personal tax preparation, GST/HST compliance, financial reporting, business structuring, forecasting, tax planning, and strategic consulting. But what clients remember isn’t the list — it’s the personalized service and the relationship. I learn how your business actually runs, and I give you advice I’d give my own company.
