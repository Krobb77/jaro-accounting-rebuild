What a real Jaro invitation looks like

A genuine invitation email from Jaro Accounting:

  • Comes from jared@jaroaccounting.com.
  • Addresses you by name and shows your email as your username.
  • Has one link: a "Set your password" button pointing to https://jaroaccounting.com/portal/accept — the link expires after 7 days.
  • Never asks for your password by email or phone. We'll never ask.

If an email asks you to sign in anywhere else, or asks for your password, don't click — contact Jared Robbins directly at jared@jaroaccounting.com or 1-(778)-227-4891.

Locked out?

If you've lost access to your authenticator app, contact Jared and he can reset your two-step verification after verifying your identity.
